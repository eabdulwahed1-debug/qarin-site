LiDAR or camera robot vacuum?

Navigation type decides whether the robot cleans your home in an orderly way or wanders randomly. Here are the main differences.

CriterionLiDAR (laser)Camera
MappingAccurate and fastGood in recent models
Working in the darkExcellentAffected by low light
Avoiding small obstacles (cables, socks)Relies on extra sensorsAdvanced models recognize them
HeightThe laser turret makes it a bit tallerUsually slimmer, fits under lower furniture
PrivacyTakes no imagesA camera inside your home — review privacy settings
Bottom line: for most multi-room homes, LiDAR is the more reliable choice. Choose a camera if your furniture is very low, or if kids' toys and cables often cover the floor, with a model that recognizes obstacles.
