Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-31

Data we collect

We don't ask you to create an account and we don't collect personal data. We use the analytics tool GoatCounter to count visits, the most-read pages and clicks on store links, in aggregate only. It does not use cookies, does not track you across websites, and does not store your IP address.

Store links

When you click a store link (such as Amazon), you go to that store's website. What happens there — including the cookies used to track referral commissions — is governed by that store's privacy policy.

Third-party services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts, illustrative photos from Unsplash, YouTube video thumbnails are shown, and the YouTube player (privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie mode) only loads when you click a video; these services may log basic technical data such as IP addresses according to their own policies.
