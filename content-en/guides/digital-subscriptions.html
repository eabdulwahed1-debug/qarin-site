Digital subscriptions: worth the money?

A monthly subscription looks cheap, but five forgotten ones add up to a big amount by year-end. This guide helps you pick the subscriptions you'll actually use.

1. Ask yourself 3 questions before subscribing

  1. How often will I use it each week? If less than once, you probably don't need it.
  2. Is there a free option that's enough? Many tools have a good free plan.
  3. Does it save me time or make me money? Those are the easiest subscriptions to justify.

2. The most useful types of subscription

TypeWho it suitsTip
Cloud storagePeople with lots of photos and filesPick the service tied to your phone for automatic backup
Design toolsSmall businesses and content creatorsTry the free plan first and upgrade when needed
AI toolsWriting, research and daily workSubscribe to one tool you use daily instead of several
Learning platforms and coursesBuilding skills and career growthAnnual is cheaper if you're committed; otherwise buy just the course you need
Audiobooks and e-booksReading while driving or exercisingCheck there's enough content in your language before subscribing

3. How to avoid forgotten subscriptions

  • When you start a free trial, set a reminder two days before it ends.
  • Review your subscriptions in your phone's app store settings once a month.
  • Paying yearly is cheaper — but only for a subscription you've used for at least two months in a row.
