How to choose your next phone

Your phone is the device you use most, and the difference between models isn't always about price. Focus on what matters to you, not the biggest number in the ad.

1. Start with how you use it

User typeFocus onDon't overpay for
Messaging and social mediaGood battery, clear screen, enough storageThe most powerful chip
Photos and content creationMain camera, video stabilization, storageMany extra low-resolution lenses
GamingProcessor, high refresh-rate screen, coolingExtra cameras
Older adultsLarge screen, loud speaker, ease of useFlagship specs

2. Specs that actually make a difference

  • Storage: photos, videos and messaging apps fill space fast; 256 GB is comfortable for most people, 128 GB if you rely on cloud storage.
  • Battery: capacity (mAh) is a hint, but chip and screen efficiency matter just as much; read user reviews about real battery life.
  • Years of updates: how many years of OS and security updates does the maker promise? That decides the phone's real lifespan.
  • Screen: a high refresh rate (120 Hz) makes everyday use smoother.

3. Buy with a clear warranty

  • Make sure the phone is an official version with a local warranty from the agent, not an imported unit without warranty.
  • Buy from the store itself or a trusted seller with many reviews.
  • Check support for Saudi carriers' networks and 5G if it matters to you.
Qarin's take: a recent mid-range phone is enough for most people. A flagship is worth it if you care about pro photography or heavy gaming, or keep your phone 4+ years.
