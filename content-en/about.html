About Qarin

Qarin is an independent website that publishes buying guides and comparisons for shoppers in Saudi Arabia and the Arab world, in Arabic and English. Our goal is simple: help you buy the right product for your needs the first time.

How we make our content

  • We collect specifications from manufacturers' official sources and store pages.
  • We summarize what buyers say in verified reviews, and we make clear that this is their experience, not ours.
  • We use AI tools for research and drafting, and we review the information before publishing.
  • We update guides regularly because prices and models change.

If you spot something inaccurate, we are happy to correct it — please use our contact page.
