💡 Quick tips

  • Get down to your child's eye level and see what they can reach.
  • Anchor heavy furniture and TVs to the wall.
  • Keep medicines and cleaning products in a high, locked cabinet.
  • Measure the stair or door opening before buying a gate.

⚠️ Safety products don't replace supervising your child.
