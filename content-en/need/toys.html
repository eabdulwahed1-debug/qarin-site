💡 Quick tips

  • Always check the age rating on the product.
  • Under 3: avoid small parts.
  • Play together — shared play builds language more than solo play.
  • Keep fewer toys out and rotate them to keep interest.

⚠️ Check the safety and age warnings on every toy before buying.
