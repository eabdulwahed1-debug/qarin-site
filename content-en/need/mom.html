💡 Quick tips

  • Keep feeding and changing supplies together in one nearby spot.
  • Multi-function devices (sterilize + dry) save time and space.
  • Read buyer reviews about noise before buying night-time devices.
  • Check that spare parts are available for pumps and bottles.

⚠️ For questions about feeding or your baby's health, talk to your paediatrician or a lactation consultant.
