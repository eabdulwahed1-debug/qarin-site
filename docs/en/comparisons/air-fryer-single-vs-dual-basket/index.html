Single-basket or dual-basket air fryer?

The most common question when buying an air fryer for a family. The answer depends more on how you cook than on the number of litres.

CriterionOne large basketTwo baskets
Cooking two dishes at onceNo (or with a divider on some models)Yes, with different settings per basket
Large items (whole chicken)BetterA single basket may be too small
Counter spaceUsually smallerWider
CleaningOne basketTwo baskets and two trays
PriceUsually lower for the same capacityUsually higher
Bottom line: if your meals are usually a main plus a side (chicken + fries) for a family of 5+, two baskets are more convenient. If you cook large items or want a lower price and smaller footprint, one large basket is better.
