E-reader or paper books?

If you read more than one book a month or travel often, this question is worth thinking about. These are the differences that matter.

CriterionE-reader (e-ink screen)Paper books
Eye comfortE-ink screens are unlike a phone screen, and many people find long reading comfortableNaturally comfortable
TravelA whole library in a light deviceHeavy and bulky
Arabic booksAvailable but fewer than English; check that the titles you want exist digitallyThe widest choice for Arabic books
CostThe device first, and some e-books are cheaperNo device cost
Notes and highlightsEasy search, highlighting and exporting notesBy pen — more enjoyable for some
Kids—Illustrated paper books are usually better for young children
Bottom line: an e-reader is worth it if you read a lot, travel, or mostly read in English. If you mostly read Arabic books or love paper, stick with paper — or combine both.

If you choose an e-reader

  • An adjustable front light for night reading.
  • Water resistance if you read by the pool or beach.
  • Enough storage if you read comics or manga.
