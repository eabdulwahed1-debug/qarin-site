💡 Quick tips

  • Talk to your child a lot and describe what you're doing in short, clear sentences.
  • Read a story every day and let them name pictures and finish words.
  • Use picture cards as a game, not homework — a few minutes a day is enough.
  • Cut screen time and add more talking and shared play.

⚠️ If you notice speech is delayed compared with peers, or you're worried for any reason, see a paediatrician or speech therapist early — these tools support practice and are not treatment.
