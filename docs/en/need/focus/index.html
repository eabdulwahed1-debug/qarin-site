💡 Quick tips

  • Break tasks into short steps and use a visual timer for each one.
  • Hang a picture routine chart so your child can see what comes next.
  • Set up a quiet homework corner away from the TV and noise.
  • Praise effort and small wins.

⚠️ If attention problems persist and affect school or daily life, see a paediatrician or specialist — these are organisation aids, not treatment.
