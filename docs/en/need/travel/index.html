💡 Quick tips

  • Pack a small bag with quiet toys, snacks and a full change of clothes within reach.
  • Check your stroller's folded size and the airline's policy before you fly.
  • Download stories or songs before the trip and use volume-limited headphones.
  • Let your child pick one new toy to open on the plane.

⚠️ Always check the safety, age and weight guidance printed on car seats and strollers.
