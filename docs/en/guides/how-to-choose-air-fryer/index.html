How to choose an air fryer for your family

Air fryers are one of the most popular kitchen appliances in Saudi Arabia, yet many people buy the wrong size or pay for features they never use. This guide boils the decision down to five points.

1. Size: start with how many people you cook for

Capacity (in litres) is the most important decision. Here is a common rule of thumb to start from:

PeopleApproximate capacityNote
1–22–3 litresEnough for a small meal or sides
3–44–6 litresThe most popular size for small families
5+7 litres or more, or two basketsGood for gatherings and Ramadan meals

Food needs space for hot air to circulate; a packed basket gives less crispy results. If you're between two sizes, go bigger.

2. Single or dual basket?

  • One large basket: better for a whole chicken and large pieces, and usually cheaper.
  • Dual basket: cook two dishes at once at different settings, and some models finish them together. Great for big families, but each basket is smaller on its own.

3. Wattage and power

Most home air fryers range roughly from 1,300 to 2,400 watts; higher wattage heats larger sizes faster. Make sure the unit is rated for the 220–240 V used in Saudi Arabia and that the plug fits or comes with an approved adapter.

4. Easy cleaning

Buyers mention this a lot in reviews. Look for:

  • A dishwasher-safe basket and tray.
  • A good non-stick coating — and avoid metal utensils on it.
  • A viewing window is nice, but it needs extra cleaning.

5. Features not worth paying much for

  • Dozens of presets: most people set temperature and time manually.
  • App and Wi-Fi control: handy for some, but it raises the price without improving cooking.

Quick checklist before you buy

  • Capacity matches your household (go bigger if unsure).
  • 220–240 V, with a warranty from the official agent or a trusted seller.
  • Dishwasher-safe basket.
  • Reviews from real buyers (be wary of very few or very similar reviews).
  • Sold and shipped by the store itself (e.g. "Ships from Amazon") for easy returns.
